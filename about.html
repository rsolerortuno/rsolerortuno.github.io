Experience & approach

I work between the biology and the decision.

My background covers antibody immunotherapies, precision oncology, developmental neurobiology and building computational tools. The job has been the same in all of them: take a vague question, pull evidence from whatever modalities are available, and say plainly what the data supports and what it does not.

Profile

Computational biologist with 6+ years across industry and academia.

I have a PhD in bioinformatics and two years of postdoctoral work in immuno-oncology and tumour biology, using single-cell and spatial transcriptomics, multi-omic integration and machine learning for biomarker discovery, validation and patient stratification. In industry that has meant antibody immunotherapies against TNFR2 and FcγRIIB, mode-of-action studies, and translational work in oncology and autoimmune disease.

The useful part is that I know the biology and can also write the code. I can pick up a question at experimental design and QC and carry it through integration, modelling and interpretation to something an experimental or clinical team can use. The pipelines are R and Python, they run on HPC and AWS, and they are meant to be rerun by someone who is not me.

I have worked in Spain, Germany, the United Kingdom and Sweden, and I am comfortable presenting to people whose background is nothing like mine.

Expression turned out to be restricted to M1 macrophages, inflammatory monocytes and neutrophils.
